Iyunga Secondary.

We provide a safe, loving, and nurturing environment where every student is encouraged to learn, play, and grow with confidence. Through engaging activities, compassionate care, and a strong sense of community, we help young minds develop the skills, values, and happiness they need to build a bright future.

Our Core values

  1. Safe and Nurturing Environment

    We provide a secure, welcoming, and caring space where every student feels protected, valued, and encouraged to explore the world with confidence.

  2. Learning Through Play

    We believe students learn best through fun and engaging activities that stimulate creativity, curiosity, problem-solving, and social development.

  3. Love, Care and Respect

    We treat every student with kindness, compassion, and dignity, fostering positive relationships built on trust, empathy, and mutual respect.

  4. Growing Together in Common

    We partner with families and the wider community to create a supportive environment where students can develop a sense of belonging, cooperation, and shared responsibility.
